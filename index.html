MALAYSIAN SELLER GUIDE & COMMON QUESTIONS

      
Everything you need to know before uploading to MyInvois


      

        

          
How do Shopee & Lazada sellers submit monthly e-Invoices?

          

            If buyers didn't request an individual e-Invoice during the month, LHDN lets you combine all completed B2C orders into a Monthly Consolidated e-Invoice due within 7 calendar days after month-end (e.g., Oct orders due by Nov 7). Export your Shopee/Lazada order CSV, drop it above, and upload the downloaded file to myinvois.hasil.gov.my.
          

        

        

          
Why did MyInvois reject my file with Error ERR236?

          

            ERR236 happens when you use the General Public TIN (EI00000000010) without setting the line classification code to 004 (Consolidated e-Invoice), or when buyer registration fields are left empty instead of NA. This tool fixes all three automatically.
          

        

        

          
What happens if a customer bought more than RM 10,000 in one order?

          

            LHDN prohibits consolidating any single receipt above RM 10,000.00 under code 004. Our converter automatically spots any order above RM 10,000 and splits it into a separate standalone document so your batch won't get rejected.
          

        

        

          
Is my Shopee customer or sales revenue stored on your server?

          

            Never. CSV parsing and cell repair previews run directly inside your web browser, and final Excel/XML compilation executes in stateless RAM—zero customer names, TINs, or sales rows are ever logged or saved to any database (PDPA safe).
