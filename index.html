COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked by Malaysian sellers & accountants

When is the monthly consolidated e-Invoice deadline for Shopee & retail sellers? +

Under LHDN's e-Invoice guidelines, B2C transactions where the customer did not request an individual e-Invoice can be combined into a Monthly Consolidated e-Invoice (Classification 004) submitted within 7 calendar days after the end of the month (for example, October orders must be submitted on or before November 7).

Do I need LHDN ERP API credentials or technical setup to use this? +

No API keys or IT setup are needed. MyInvois Batch Doctor generates the exact 11-Sheet Batch Excel (.xls) and PEPPOL UBL 2.1 (.xml) files accepted directly by LHDN's free MyInvois Portal (myinvois.hasil.gov.my).

What MSIC code should Shopee, Lazada, or TikTok Shop sellers enter? +

Most Malaysian online marketplace sellers use MSIC 47912 (Retail sale of any kind of product over the Internet). Cafes and restaurants typically use 56101, mini markets use 47111, and clothing retailers use 47711—all available as 1-click presets inside the Converter Workspace.

Can I use one RM 29 Lifetime License for multiple shops or client companies? +

Yes. Your Lifetime License unlocks unlimited batch exports inside the Converter Workspace with no restriction on Supplier TIN or SSM BRN—making it ideal for serial shop owners, company secretaries, and freelance bookkeepers.
