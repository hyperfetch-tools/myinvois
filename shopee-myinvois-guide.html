Malaysia e-Invoice Practical Guide (2026)

How to Submit Shopee, Lazada & TikTok Shop Monthly Orders to LHDN MyInvois

Under LHDN's e-Invoice guidelines, Malaysian online sellers do not need to key in hundreds of individual Shopee or TikTok Shop parcels one by one—unless a buyer specifically requested a tax e-Invoice during that month. Instead, you can submit a Monthly Consolidated e-Invoice within 7 calendar days after the end of each month (for example, October sales must be submitted by November 7).

2. Step-by-Step Monthly Workflow (Takes 2 Minutes)

  1. Export Completed Orders: In Shopee Seller Centre, go to My Orders → Completed → Export for the previous calendar month.
  2. Watch the RM 10,000 Rule: Under LHDN Specific Guideline Section 3.6, any single receipt exceeding RM 10,000.00 cannot be included inside a consolidated 004 e-Invoice—it must be separated as an individual document.
  3. Convert to 11-Sheet MyInvois Excel or XML: Open MyInvois Batch Doctor, enter your company TIN, 12-digit SSM number, and MSIC 47912, and drop your Shopee CSV.
  4. Upload to MyInvois Portal: Log in to myinvois.hasil.gov.my, open Batch Submission, and upload the generated file.
